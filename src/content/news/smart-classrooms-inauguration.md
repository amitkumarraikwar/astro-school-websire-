M.P. Public School, Indore is delighted to announce the inauguration of 20 new state-of-the-art smart classrooms, equipped with the latest interactive technology to transform the teaching and learning experience.

## Features of the New Smart Classrooms

- **Interactive whiteboards** with touch-screen capability
- **High-definition projectors** for clear visual presentations
- **Digital learning software** integrated with MP Board curriculum
- **Audio-visual systems** for multimedia lessons
- **Ergonomic furniture** designed for student comfort
- **High-speed Wi-Fi** connectivity in all classrooms

## Investment in the Future

The school management has invested over ₹50 lakhs in this infrastructure upgrade as part of its ongoing commitment to providing world-class education facilities in Indore.

"Technology is not a replacement for teachers but a powerful tool that enhances the learning experience," said Mrs. Shraddha Pardeshi, Principal. "These smart classrooms will enable our teachers to make lessons more engaging and interactive."

## Training for Teachers

All faculty members have undergone a comprehensive two-week training program to effectively utilize the new technology in their teaching methodology. The training was conducted by certified EdTech professionals.

## Benefits for Students

- Enhanced understanding through visual and interactive content
- Access to vast digital libraries and educational resources
- Preparation for a technology-driven future
- More engaging and participatory classroom sessions
