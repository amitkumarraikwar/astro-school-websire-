The Annual Sports Day of M.P. Public School, Indore was held on February 20, 2025, at the school sports complex. The event witnessed enthusiastic participation from over 800 students across all classes.

## Event Highlights

The day began with a march past by all four houses – Ganga, Yamuna, Narmada, and Kaveri – followed by the traditional torch relay and oath-taking ceremony.

### Track & Field Events
- 100m, 200m, 400m, and relay races
- Long jump, high jump, and shot put
- Obstacle race for junior students

### Team Sports Exhibition
- Cricket demonstration match
- Basketball inter-house final
- Volleyball exhibition match

## Chief Guest

The event was graced by Shri Ramesh Agrawal, former national-level athlete and sports consultant, who distributed prizes to the winners and encouraged students to pursue sports alongside academics.

## House Championship Results

1. **Narmada House** – Overall Champions (520 points)
2. **Ganga House** – Runners Up (485 points)
3. **Kaveri House** – Second Runners Up (460 points)
4. **Yamuna House** – (440 points)

The event concluded with a vote of thanks by Principal Dr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, who emphasized the importance of physical fitness and sportsmanship in a student's overall development.
