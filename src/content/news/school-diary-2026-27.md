The M.P. Public School diary is available on this website for parents and guardians to consult.

The document includes:

- Student profile and parent-information sections for enrolment
- School uniform rules and the four-house system
- School-published annual examination result lists for 2025–26
- The academic calendar from June 2026 to April 2027
- Class-specific examination and project schedules

[Read the school diary and academic calendar (PDF)](/documents/mpps-academic-calendar-2026-27.pdf).

Calendar dates and class arrangements can be updated by the school. Please follow current school notices and contact the office before making plans based on a diary entry. Individual results in the diary are historical records, not a promise of future performance.

For questions about the diary, fees, or a campus visit, [contact the school office](/contact).
